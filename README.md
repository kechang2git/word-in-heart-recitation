# Word in Heart / 藏在心裡

A private, offline-first PWA for memorizing and reciting your own English or Traditional Chinese Bible verses.

## Open the app

[Launch Word in Heart](https://kechang2git.github.io/word-in-heart-recitation/)

On iPhone or iPad, open the link in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## How to use it

1. Choose your interface language on the welcome screen.
2. Select **Add manually** to enter a Bible reference and verse text, or select **Import PDF** to extract verses from a monthly PDF.
3. Carefully review imported wording before saving it. Nothing from a PDF is saved until you confirm it.
4. Select **Start practice** and move through the cue levels:
   - **Full** — displays the complete verse.
   - **Light cloze** — hides a few words.
   - **Deep cloze** — hides more words.
   - **Initials** — displays English word initials or the first Chinese character of each phrase.
   - **Blank** — hides the verse completely.
5. Type or speak the verse from memory, compare it with the saved wording, and choose **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy** to schedule the next review.
6. Use **Monthly Plan** to view your verses and **Progress** to review your practice history.

## Backup and privacy

Your verses and progress stay in this browser and are not uploaded to an app server. Use **Settings → Download JSON backup** regularly. You can restore that file on another device or after clearing browser data.

The app does not include a Bible translation. Please enter or import only text you have permission to use.
